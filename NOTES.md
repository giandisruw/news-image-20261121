## Stories used in the image

### 1. Russian missiles land in Poland (LEAD)
- Fri 20 Nov, overnight: two Russian missiles and a suspected jet-powered
  drone came down in south-east Poland during a huge Russian attack on
  Ukraine. A Kh-101 cruise missile fell near Nowy Lipowiec, about 50 km
  from the Ukraine border; a second missile near Rudnik nad Sanem; a
  probable jet-powered drone at Molodycz. No one in Poland was hurt and no
  infrastructure was destroyed.
- PM Donald Tusk: "It seems we are dealing with an intentional provocation";
  Defence Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz called the three objects a
  provocation. NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte called it a "grave
  violation" of Polish airspace. Polish and allied jets (F-16s, Swedish
  Gripens) scrambled; Patriot batteries were active, but no shots were
  fired — poor weather blocked visual identification.
- The same overnight attack on Ukraine: President Zelenskyy said Russia
  fired more than 400 drones and 110 missiles, "one of the largest attacks"
  since the war began; at least eight people were killed.
- Sources: Reuters via EuropeSays (20 Nov), AP via EuropeSays (20 Nov),
  SOFREP (20 Nov), Nexus Europa (20 Nov).

### 2. Wall Street slumps into correction
- Fri 20 Nov: the S&P 500 fell 3.7% (265.44 points, to 6,910.39) and the
  Dow 1.9% (949.73 points, to 48,147.33); both are now more than 10% below
  their recent peaks — correction territory. The Nasdaq sank 5.3%
  (1,261.47 points, to 22,617.73) and is down for the year; worst week
  since March 2020 (S&P -8.6%, Dow -8%, Nasdaq -10% for the week).
- AI sell-off: Nvidia fell 14% on Friday (down 23% for the week, ~50%
  below its 6 Oct record high); AMD -13%, Broadcom -12%, Micron -14%.
  Anthropic fell about 9% to ~$88.30, a day after its record IPO.
- Sources: Investopedia (20 Nov), Wall Street Journal (20 Nov), NPR via
  NPR Illinois (20 Nov), Morningstar (20 Nov), Motley Fool (20 Nov).

### 3. Cyclone Akvan comes ashore in Odisha
- Fri 20 Nov morning: Cyclone Akvan completed landfall near Gopalpur on
  the Odisha coast (landfall process 08:00–11:20 IST), crossing with
  sustained winds of 70–80 km/h gusting to 90 km/h, then weakening into a
  deep depression. About 370,000 people (3.7 lakh) had been evacuated to
  shelters; 41+ trains cancelled.
- Odisha officials reported no major damage and no loss of life so far;
  heavy rain caused waterlogging in Berhampur and Digapahandi. Third
  cyclone on this coast in under a month (after Arnab and Murasu).
- Sources: India Meteorological Department bulletins (20 Nov), The Hindu
  BusinessLine (20 Nov), Particle News (20 Nov), JagoAmerica (20 Nov),
  Northeast Herald (20 Nov).

### 4. Denmark foils alleged Russian assassination plot
- Fri 20 Nov: Denmark's security service PET said it thwarted an attempt
  by Russian intelligence services to assassinate US-Ukrainian rapper
  Kyivstoner (Albert Vasiliev) ahead of a concert north of Copenhagen on
  25 September. Three men with links to the banned gang Loyal to Familia
  were arrested in a car carrying two loaded pistols and two knives, five
  minutes before the concert was due to begin.
- Two Danish men appeared in court on Friday, charged with attempted murder
  and assisting a foreign intelligence service; both pleaded not guilty.
  Denmark is expelling Russia's defence attache within seven days and is
  considering suspending Schengen visas for Russians.
- Sources: Reuters (20 Nov), CNN (20 Nov), Al Jazeera via EuropeSays
  (21 Nov), DR via EuropeSays (20 Nov).

### 5. Tanker hit and on fire in the Strait of Hormuz
- Fri 20 Nov ~15:00 UTC: a tanker sailing outbound through the Strait of
  Hormuz was struck by an unknown projectile eight nautical miles north of
  Oman's Khasab; the company security officer reported a fire on board.
  UKMTO issued Warning 214-26. No casualties or environmental impact
  reported. Iran's Revolutionary Guards said the tanker hit a mine — not
  independently verified. Hormuz transits down 15% week on week.
- Sources: Reuters (20 Nov), MarineLink (20 Nov), ShipUniverse (20 Nov),
  Baird Maritime (20 Nov).

### 6. UK and EU seal post-Brexit reset
- Fri 20 Nov, Brussels: Britain and the EU signed deals easing food trade,
  linking emissions trading systems and creating a youth mobility scheme.
  Food deal (from March 2028) removes certificates and checks; Britain
  estimates up to £5.1bn a year. Each side will allow 100,000 young people
  a year (aged 18–31) to work or study for up to three years, from 2030.
  PM Andy Burnham called the package a "win-win"; von der Leyen called it
  a "turning point". Tuition-fee deal deferred for six months.
- Sources: Reuters via Euronext (20 Nov), Reuters explainer (20 Nov),
  The Oldham Times (20 Nov).

### 7. Pancreatic cancer pill shrinks tumours in early trial
- Fri 20 Nov: Erasca said its experimental pill ERAS-0015 shrank tumours
  in 44% of 25 pancreatic cancer patients (11 of 25) on a 32 mg daily dose
  in the early-stage AURORAS-1 trial; in the two most common genetic
  variations the response rate was 69%. In lung cancer, 3 of 5 patients
  responded. Results presented at the EORTC-NCI-AACR Symposium in
  Barcelona; late-stage trials planned for 2027. Early-stage, small
  numbers — the image says "early trial".
- Sources: Reuters (20 Nov), Medical Daily (20 Nov), NY Post (20 Nov,
  daraxonrasib combination data).

### 8. Palisades Fire arson verdict
- Fri 20 Nov: a Los Angeles federal jury found Jonathan Rinderknecht, 30,
  guilty on both counts — destruction of property by means of fire and
  arson affecting interstate commerce — for lighting the fire on 1 Jan
  2025 that became the Palisades Fire. It killed 12 people and destroyed
  about 6,800 structures; sentencing is set for 17 March 2027. His first
  trial in June ended in a hung jury (10–2 for acquittal).
- Sources: Reuters (20 Nov), US Department of Justice (20 Nov), CNN
  (20 Nov), AP via WVLT (20 Nov).
